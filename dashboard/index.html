Method & caveats

How to read this

Access (2SFCA v2): for each SA2 a representative point (inside the polygon, not a naive centroid) gets 15- & 30-minute driving catchments (Mapbox isochrones); LDC approved places are shared across the demand they can reach with stepped decay — full weight ≤15 min, half 15–30 min (symmetric-catchment approximation). Points that would land offshore or in farmland are moved to the SA2's closest edge to care; SA2s still with no reachable LDC within 30 minutes show as "no service". Bands are national tertiles among SA2s with ≥100 kids 0–4. LDC = long day care; preschool, OSHC and family day care stay visible separately in the register views.

Inputs: ABS ERP 2001–25 (kids 0–4, SA2); SEIFA 2021 IRSAD; AEDC 2024 SA2 tables — "developmentally vulnerable on one or more domains" (© AEDC, used per its Data Guidelines); ACECQA registers (places, approved hours); StartingBlocks fees. 2030 figures extend each SA2's 5-year population trend with today's places — a scenario for planning discussion, not a forecast. Isochrones reflect typical driving conditions; public transport, traffic peaks and part-time booking patterns are not modelled. Approved hours ≠ actual operating hours.

Payloads: equity.json · sa2-access.json · hours.json · API: /api/equity, /api/sa2/:code
